We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services. The personal information we collect includes:

We do not process sensitive information. All personal information that you provide to us must be true, complete, and accurate.

We process your personal information for a variety of reasons, depending on how you interact with our Services, including:

- To respond to user inquiries and offer support.

- To protect our Services and prevent fraud.
- To evaluate and improve our Services, products, and your experience.

We only disclose your information to third-party vendors, service providers, or agents ("third parties") who perform services for us or on our behalf. The categories of third parties we may share personal information with are:
- AI Platforms (such as Google Cloud AI / Gemini for text analysis of anonymous reviews)
- Cloud Computing Services (such as Supabase for database hosting)
- Data Storage Service Providers
- User Account Registration & Authentication Services

### 4. DO WE OFFER ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE-BASED PRODUCTS?
Yes. Our application utilizes Artificial Intelligence (Google Cloud AI) strictly for Text Analysis, AI Insights, and Natural Language Processing. The AI analyzes the text of anonymous user-submitted reviews to generate insights. No personal information is processed by the AI.

### 5. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?
We keep your information for as long as you have an account with us. When you delete your account, your personal information is automatically deleted from our databases.
